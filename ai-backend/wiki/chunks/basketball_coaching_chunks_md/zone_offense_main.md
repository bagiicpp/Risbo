If you coach long enough, you’re going to see zone defenses—2-3, 1-3-1, 3-2, and everything in between. That’s why this page exists—to give you a simple, effective framework for building a zone offense that works at _any level_. Here, you’ll find:

*   Core concepts that help your players read and react vs. any zone *   Zone offense plays designed to create high-percentage shots

Once your players grasp these ideas, they’ll know how to create scoring opportunities no matter what kind of zone they face.

Whether you're teaching young kids to move without the ball, or looking for a few set plays to use in a varsity game, you’ll find what you need here.

Let’s break it down and give your team the tools to _thrive_ against any zone defense.

## **Blueprint Of Success Against Zones**

Coach Schultz provides you with a blueprint for how to attack every zone. It is a multi-layered approach to **dismantling any zone offense you might see**.

Coach Schultz doesn’t leave anything to chance. His system has 4 phases to help guarantee you success against zones.

### **Phase One:****Sets and Quick Hitters To Destroy Confidence**

Over the years, Coach Schultz learned the easiest and fastest way to get teams out of zones is by attacking them with some unstoppable sets.

**If you score easy buckets the first 2 or 3 times against the zone, 90% of the teams pull out of their zone immediately.** This is why Coach Schultz has put such an emphasis on using proven sets to get easy buckets.

Coach Schultz teaches you how to implement these sets as well as which sets work best against odd and even front zones. You’ll have 20+ Sets and Baseline Out Of Bounds plays to choose from.

And the sets don’t just give an open look for a 3. They almost always provide an easy look at the rim for a layup or dunk.

### **Phase Two:****Universal Zone Concepts**

Coach Schultz knows it is tough to prepare for every different zone out there and the hundreds of slight variations.

So he has found it more effective to teach players Zone Concepts that work against all zones defenses. This allows your team to play conceptual zone offense as you attack the zone.

Just a few of the zone concepts covered include:

🠦**Exploiting Gaps/Seams** Learn to do this in a variety of ways, including vertical and horizontal stretching of the defense as well as quick ball reversals.

🠦**Distorting Defenses** Learn how to force defensive “forward coverage” on wings, creating 2v1 scoring opportunities.

🠦**Screening Zones** Learn to create paint touches from flare screen actions and learn to screen the interior of the zone for layups.

🠦**Blind Cutting & Playing Behind The Zone** Learn how to effectively catch in scoring position by blind cutting behind the zone as well as playing behind the defense on the baseline.

All of the concepts Coach Schultz teaches are universal. They work against any zone. So no matter what action your players will attack with a higher basketball IQ.
