# Skills and Drills for Players - USA Basketball

## Introductory Level

If you have just started playing the game (Suggested Ages: 3-9)

## Foundational Level

If you have experience and need to establish fundamental skills (Suggested Ages: 8-13)

## Advanced Level

If you play on travel, middle school or high school teams (Suggested Ages: 12-17)

## Performance Level

For the highest-level playing resources (Suggested Ages: 15-18+)
